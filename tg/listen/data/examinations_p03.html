BARIUM MEAL ANATOMY

Left Lateral Position: Drinking the Bariumالوضعية الأولى: الجانب الأيسر وشرب الباريوم

Left Lateral Position: Drinking the Barium

The patient then drinks the barium while lying on the left side, supported by their elbow. This position prevents the barium from reaching the duodenum too quickly.

Supine and Slightly on the Right Side: Checking for Refluxالوضعية الثانية: الاستلقاء والميلان إلى اليمين وفحص الارتجاع

Supine and Slightly on the Right Side: Checking for Reflux

The patient then lies supine and slightly on the right side, to bring the barium up against the gastro-esophageal junction to check for reflux, which may be revealed by asking the patient to cough or to swallow water while in this position (the ‘water siphon’ test).

Rolling in a Complete Circle: The RAO Positionالوضعية الثالثة: التدحرج الكامل وإسقاط RAO

Rolling in a Complete Circle: The RAO Position

The patient is asked to roll onto the right side and then quickly over in a complete circle, to finish in an RAO position. This roll is performed to coat the gastric mucosa with barium.

Intravenous Smooth Muscle Relaxantالحقن الوريدي لمرخٍّ للعضلات الملساء

Intravenous Smooth Muscle Relaxant

An i.v. injection of a smooth muscle relaxant (Buscopan 20 mg or glucagon 0.3 mg) may be given to better distend the stomach and to slow down the emptying of contrast into duodenum.

Note: Administration of Buscopan has been shown to not affect the detection of gastro-esophageal reflux or hiatus hernia.
