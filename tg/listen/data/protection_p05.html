Classification of exposure

Radiation protection for Classification of exposure

According to the recommendations of the International Commission on Radiological Protection (ICRP), the primary classification of exposure in radiation protection includes:

Occupational exposure

Medical exposure

Public exposure

Each category requires a different approach radiation protection.

Occupational Exposureالتعرّض المهني

Occupational Exposure

1.Occupational Exposure

It includes people who work with or near radiation sources, such as doctors, radiologic technologists, nuclear plant workers, and industrial radiographers.

Protection measures (occupational exposure)إجراءات الوقاية للتعرّض المهني

Protection measures (occupational exposure)

Protection measures

Time: Minimize the time spent near radiation sources.

Distance: Increase the distance from the radiation source whenever possible.

Shielding: Use appropriate structural shielding and personal protective equipment, such as lead aprons.

Personal dosimetry: Wear personal 4 dosimeters according to the facility's radiation protection program.

ALARA principleمبدأ ALARA

ALARA principle

ALARA

Principle Minimizing Radiation Exposure

Reduce time: Less time spent near source. less radiation received.

Increase distance: Greater distance from source. less radiation received.

Increase shielding: Behind shielding from source. less radiation received.

Medical Exposureالتعرّض الطبي

Medical Exposure

2.Medical Exposure

Medical exposure is the exposure to ionizing radiation associated with medical diagnosis or treatment.

It primarily includes patients undergoing X-ray imaging, CT scans, fluoroscopy, nuclear medicine examinations, and radiation therapy.

Medical exposure may also include people who assist patients circumstances .

Principles of medical radiation protectionمبادئ الوقاية الإشعاعية الطبية

Principles of medical radiation protection

Principles of medical radiation protection

Every medical examination involving ionizing radiation should be justified by determining whether the expected clinical benefit outweighs the potential radiation risk.

The radiation dose should be optimized to obtain the required diagnostic information or therapeutic outcome while avoiding unnecessary exposure.

Public Exposureتعرّض عامة الناس

Public Exposure

3. Public Exposure

Public exposure refers to exposure to ionizing radiation received by members of the general public from radiation sources, excluding occupational and medical exposure.

It may result from environmental releases, radiation facilities, radioactive waste .

Protection measures (public exposure)إجراءات الوقاية لتعرّض عامة الناس

Protection measures (public exposure)

Protection measures

Shielding and facility design: Use appropriate barriers and structural shielding to prevent unnecessary exposure.

Control of radioactive materials: Ensure safe storage, handling, transport, and disposal.

Environmental monitoring: Monitor radiation levels around relevant facilities and assess potential public exposure.

Access control: Restrict access to controlled radiation areas.
