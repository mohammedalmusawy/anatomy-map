X – Ray tube consists of four major components:

يتكوّن أنبوب الأشعة السينية من أربعة مكوّنات رئيسية:

  1. 1. Cathodeالكاثود (القطب السالب).
  2. 2. Anodeالأنود (القطب الموجب).
  3. 3. Glass Envelopeالغلاف الزجاجي.
  4. 4. Protective housingالغطاء الواقي.
الفكرة المهمةأنبوب الأشعة = كاثود + أنود + غلاف زجاجي + غطاء واقٍ.Four components: cathode, anode, glass envelope, protective housing.

الكاثود: الفتيلةCathode: The Filament

The cathode is the negative side of the x-ray tube because it is a source of electrons and has two primary parts: filament & focusing cup.

الكاثود هو الجانب السالب من أنبوب الأشعة لأنه مصدر الإلكترونات، وله جزءان أساسيان: الفتيلة (filament) وكأس التركيز (focusing cup).

Is a coil of wire usually about 2 mm in diameter and 1 to 2 cm long . An x – ray filament emit electrons when it heated. When the current through the filament sufficiently intense, approximately 4 A and above , the outer – shell electrons of the filament atoms are literally boiled off and ejected from the filament. this phenomenon is known as thermionic emission.

الفتيلة ملف من سلك قطره عادةً حوالي 2 ملم وطوله 1–2 سم، تبعث الإلكترونات عند تسخينها. عندما يكون التيار المارّ فيها شديداً بما يكفي، حوالي 4 أمبير فأكثر، «تغلي» إلكترونات المدار الخارجي لذرّات الفتيلة وتنطلق منها، وهذه الظاهرة تُسمّى الانبعاث الحراري للإلكترونات (thermionic emission).

Filaments are usually made of thoriated tungsten, because its melting point is 3410 C°, and therefore it is not likely to burn-out. Also, tungsten doesn’t vaporize easily, if it did, the tube would quickly become gassy and its internal parts coated with tungsten.

الفتائل تُصنع عادةً من التنگستن المعالَج بالثوريوم (thoriated tungsten)، لأن درجة انصهاره 3410 °م فلا يُرجَّح أن يحترق. كما أن التنگستن لا يتبخّر بسهولة؛ ولو تبخّر لصار الأنبوب غازياً بسرعة وتغطّت أجزاؤه الداخلية بالتنگستن.

This is the most common cause of tube failure, the addition of 1% to 2% thorium to the tungsten filament increases efficiency of thermionic emission and prolongs tube life.

وهذا (تبخّر التنگستن) هو أشيع سبب لعطل الأنبوب. وإضافة 1%–2% ثوريوم إلى فتيلة التنگستن تزيد كفاءة الانبعاث الحراري وتطيل عمر الأنبوب.

Fig. (2) shows a magnified view of the cathode face and it shows dual filaments. Only one filament at a time will work. The small filament is designed to be used with relatively small parts while the large filament is used when larger body parts are being x-rayed.

الشكل (2) يبيّن صورة مكبّرة لوجه الكاثود بفتيلتين (dual filaments). تعمل فتيلة واحدة فقط في كل مرة: الفتيلة الصغيرة للأجزاء الصغيرة نسبياً، والكبيرة عند تصوير أجزاء الجسم الكبيرة.

The number of electrons that are released will directly influence the number of x-rays that are generated and therefore the dose of radiation also. Figure (3) shows how a low mA will result in fewer x-rays being produced and a high mA will result in a greater number of x-rays. The term mA is a unit of measure of electrical current that is used when the technologist selects the exposure factors.

عدد الإلكترونات المتحرّرة يؤثّر مباشرةً في عدد الأشعة المنتَجة، وبالتالي في جرعة الإشعاع أيضاً. الشكل (3) يبيّن أن mA المنخفض ينتج أشعة أقل، وmA العالي ينتج أشعة أكثر. وmA وحدة قياس التيار الكهربائي التي يستعملها المصوّر عند اختيار عوامل التعرّض.

By referring to the types of the filaments, the small filament directs its electron stream to the small focus while using the large filament, directs the stream over the larger focal spot. The small focal spot is superimposed over the large one.

الفتيلة الصغيرة توجّه تيار إلكتروناتها إلى البؤرة الصغيرة، والكبيرة توجّهه إلى البؤرة الكبيرة. والبؤرة الصغيرة متراكبة فوق الكبيرة.

الفكرة المهمةالفتيلة (تنگستن + 1–2% ثوريوم) تبعث الإلكترونات بالتسخين (thermionic emission)؛ عدد الإلكترونات = mA.The thoriated-tungsten filament emits electrons by thermionic emission; their number is set by mA.

الكاثود: كأس التركيزCathode: The Focusing Cup

When the x-ray machine is powered up, electrons will literally “boil” off the filament. It glows white hot and the electrons hover around the filament in a “space charge” until the moment of exposure and then they accelerate very rapidly towards the anode which is not very far away. This process is known as “thermionic emission”. Thermionic emission occurs when the technologist begins to make an exposure by pressing the “ready” button on the machine.

عند تشغيل الجهاز «تغلي» الإلكترونات من الفتيلة حرفياً؛ تتوهّج الفتيلة بلون أبيض وتحوم الإلكترونات حولها في «شحنة فراغية» (space charge) حتى لحظة التعرّض، ثم تتسارع بسرعة كبيرة نحو الأنود القريب. هذه العملية تُسمّى الانبعاث الحراري للإلكترونات، وتحدث عندما يبدأ المصوّر التعرّض بضغط زر «ready».

The filament is embedded in a metal shroud called the focusing cup to eliminate the space charge and concentrate the electron stream in a pre-determined area on the anode target area known as a “focal spot”. Since all the electrons accelerated from cathode to anode are electrically negative, the beam tend to spread out owing to electrostatic repulsion and some electrons can even miss the anode completely , the focusing cup is negatively charged so that it condenses the electron beam to a small area of the anode, Figure (4) .The filament has its own circuit powered by a relatively low voltage and 4 – 6 amperes.

الفتيلة مغروسة في غلاف معدني اسمه كأس التركيز، لإزالة الشحنة الفراغية وتركيز تيار الإلكترونات في منطقة محدّدة من هدف الأنود اسمها «البؤرة» (focal spot). ولأن كل الإلكترونات سالبة، تميل الحزمة إلى الانتشار بسبب التنافر الكهربائي، وقد تفوت بعضها الأنود كلياً؛ لذلك يكون كأس التركيز مشحوناً سلبياً فيجمع الحزمة في منطقة صغيرة من الأنود (الشكل 4). وللفتيلة دائرتها الخاصة بجهد منخفض نسبياً و4–6 أمبير.

الفكرة المهمةكأس التركيز سالب الشحنة: يمنع انتشار الإلكترونات ويركّزها على البؤرة.The negatively charged focusing cup condenses the electron beam onto the focal spot.

الأنود والهدفAnode & Target

The anode is the positive side of the x-ray tube, which divided into two types:

الأنود هو الجانب الموجب من أنبوب الأشعة، وهو نوعان:

  • • Stationary – anodes that are used in dental x-ray machine, some portable machines and other special purpose units in which high tube current and power are not required.الأنود الثابت: يُستعمل في أجهزة الأسنان وبعض الأجهزة المحمولة والوحدات الخاصة التي لا تحتاج تيار أنبوب وقدرة عاليين.
  • • Rotating – anode which are used in general purpose x – ray tubes because they must be capable of producing high – intensity x – ray beams in a short time.الأنود الدوّار: يُستعمل في أنابيب الأشعة العامة لأنها يجب أن تنتج حزماً عالية الشدة في وقت قصير.

The rotor is actually part of a motor that is made to rotate at very high speed. In most x-ray tubes, the revolutions per minute is usually at 3,200 for standard technique settings, however if the technologist selects exposure factors that are considerably larger and therefore very hot, then the rotor can rotate at a much greater speed exceeding 5,000 rpm.

الدوّار (rotor) جزء من محرّك مصمّم ليدور بسرعة عالية جداً. في أغلب الأنابيب تكون السرعة عادةً 3200 دورة/دقيقة لعوامل التعرّض الاعتيادية، لكن إذا اختار المصوّر عوامل أكبر بكثير (حرارة عالية جداً) يدور بسرعة أكبر تتجاوز 5000 دورة/دقيقة.

The rotation of the anode ensures that not any one spot will receive successive pulses of electrons. If there was no rotation, then it is very likely that the anode face would be damage due to high heat.

دوران الأنود يضمن ألّا تستقبل بقعة واحدة نبضات الإلكترونات المتتالية؛ ولولا الدوران لتضرّر وجه الأنود غالباً من الحرارة العالية.

Rotating the anode allows a large surface area over which heat spread, providing an ability to tolerate greater heat deposition and to produce more x-ray photons per unit time compared with a fixed anode.

الدوران يوزّع الحرارة على مساحة كبيرة، فيتحمّل الأنود حرارة أكثر وينتج فوتونات أكثر في وحدة الزمن مقارنةً بالأنود الثابت.

الهدفTarget

The target is the area of the anode struck by the electrons from the cathode. In stationary – anode tubes the target consists of a tungsten – alloy metal embedded in the copper anode. In rotating – anode tubes the entire rotating disc is the target. Alloying the tungsten (usually with rhenium) gives it added mechanical strength to withstand the stress of the high rotation.

الهدف هو منطقة الأنود التي تصطدم بها إلكترونات الكاثود. في الأنود الثابت يتكوّن الهدف من سبيكة تنگستن مغروسة في أنود نحاسي. وفي الأنود الدوّار يكون القرص الدوّار كله هو الهدف، وخلط التنگستن (عادةً بالرينيوم rhenium) يعطيه قوة ميكانيكية إضافية لتحمّل إجهاد الدوران العالي.

فوائد التنگستن كمادة للهدفThe benefits of using tungsten as a target material

  1. 1. Atomic number : Tungsten’s high atomic number ,74, results in higher – efficiency x – ray production and in higher – energy x – raysالعدد الذري: العدد الذري العالي للتنگستن (74) يعطي إنتاج أشعة أكفأ وأشعة أعلى طاقة.
  2. 2. Thermal conductivity : Tungsten has a thermal conductivity nearly equal to that of copper (احتمال خطأ: الأصح: أقل من نصف توصيل النحاس، مع أن بعض الكتب مثل Bushong تذكرها كما في المحاضرة) . It is therefore an efficient metal for dissipating the heat produced.التوصيل الحراري: توصيله الحراري يقارب النحاس، فهو فعّال في تبديد الحرارة.
  3. 3. High melting point: Any material , if heated sufficiently , will melt and become liquid. Tungsten has a high melting point (3410 C°) and therefore can stand up under high tube current without pitting or bubbling.درجة انصهار عالية: أي مادة تنصهر إذا سُخّنت بما يكفي؛ والتنگستن ينصهر عند 3410 °م فيتحمّل تيار الأنبوب العالي بدون تنقّر أو تفقّع.
  4. 4. Resists vaporization at high temperatures.يقاوم التبخّر بالحرارة العالية.
  5. 5. Its availability makes it cost-effective.توفّره يجعله اقتصادياً.

The anode target must be a good thermal conductor, when the electrons comprising the tube current slam into the anode, more than 99% of their kinetic energy is converted into heat. This heat must be conducted away quickly before it can melt the anode. copper is the most common anode material.

هدف الأنود يجب أن يكون موصلاً جيداً للحرارة: عندما تصطدم إلكترونات تيار الأنبوب بالأنود يتحوّل أكثر من 99% من طاقتها الحركية إلى حرارة، ويجب تصريف هذه الحرارة بسرعة قبل أن تصهر الأنود. والنحاس هو أشيع مادة للأنود.

The distance between the cathode and the rotating anode disc is very close. This is designed this way to ensure that the projectile electron stream has a reasonably good chance of arriving at the anode in a relatively tight pattern. If the electrons were permitted to spread out, then the x-ray production process would become very inefficient

المسافة بين الكاثود وقرص الأنود الدوّار قريبة جداً، وهذا مقصود حتى يصل تيار الإلكترونات إلى الأنود بنمط ضيّق نسبياً؛ ولو انتشرت الإلكترونات لصار إنتاج الأشعة غير كفء.

الفكرة المهمةالأنود الدوّار يوزّع الحرارة (3200–5000+ دورة/دقيقة)؛ والتنگستن هدف مثالي: عدد ذري 74 وانصهار 3410 °م.Rotating anodes spread heat; tungsten (Z = 74, melting point 3410 °C) is the target material.

مبدأ البؤرة الخطيةLine Focus Principle

It is the angling of the anode target that makes the effective focal spot much smaller than the actual area of interaction.

إمالة هدف الأنود هي التي تجعل البؤرة الفعّالة أصغر بكثير من منطقة التفاعل الفعلية.

Typical dimensions are nominal sizes of 1.0- to 1.2-mm (large) and 0.3- to 0.6-mm (small) focal spots, where nominal encompasses a range of focal spot sizes that are as acceptable according to manufacturer standards.

الأبعاد النموذجية: مقاسات اسمية 1.0–1.2 ملم (كبيرة) و0.3–0.6 ملم (صغيرة)، و«اسمية» تعني مدى من المقاسات المقبولة حسب معايير الشركة المصنّعة.

Ideally, the use of small focal spots is preferred to minimize geometric blurring of patient anatomy with magnification. However, the small focal area constrains x-ray tube output and heat loading factors, mainly due to heat concentrated in a small area.

من الناحية المثالية تُفضَّل البؤرة الصغيرة لتقليل الضبابية الهندسية لتشريح المريض مع التكبير. لكن البؤرة الصغيرة تحدّ من ناتج الأنبوب وتحمّله الحراري، أساساً بسبب تركّز الحرارة في مساحة صغيرة.

Larger focal spots have higher instantaneous x-ray production capacity and are preferred, as long as blurring does not adversely affect resolution.

البؤر الكبيرة لها قدرة إنتاج لحظية أعلى وتُفضَّل ما دامت الضبابية لا تؤثّر سلباً في الدقة.

The focal spots which are imbedded in the rotating anode disc are angled in such a way that when the electrons strike the anode focal track, the angle makes it easier for the x-rays to be emitted in a downward direction. However depending on the angle of the anode face, this will change the appearance of the focal spot as seen from below or where the x-ray image receptor is positioned.

البؤر في قرص الأنود الدوّار مائلة بحيث إن الإلكترونات عندما تضرب المسار البؤري (focal track) تسهّل الزاوية انبعاث الأشعة نحو الأسفل. لكن زاوية وجه الأنود تغيّر شكل البؤرة كما تُرى من الأسفل، أي من مكان مستقبِل الصورة.

The anode angle and the resulting appearance of the focal spot as it would appear from the image receptor is known as the “Line Focus Principle’.

زاوية الأنود وشكل البؤرة الناتج كما يُرى من مستقبِل الصورة يُسمّى «مبدأ البؤرة الخطية» (Line Focus Principle).

In the Fig. (7), note that as the angle of the anode decreases from 24 degrees to 12 degrees, the focal spot will appear smaller because you are looking up at it at an angle or obliquely. As the angle decreases, you actually see less of the focal spot dimensions and this will actually enhance the details recorded on the image. In general, the smaller the focal spot, the better the detail will be on the finished image.

في الشكل (7)، عندما تقل زاوية الأنود من 24 درجة إلى 12 درجة تبدو البؤرة أصغر، لأننا ننظر إليها بزاوية مائلة. كلما قلّت الزاوية نرى أبعاداً أقل من البؤرة، وهذا يحسّن التفاصيل المسجّلة على الصورة. وبشكل عام: كلما صغرت البؤرة كانت تفاصيل الصورة أفضل.

نوعا البؤرةTwo types of focal spot

  • • Actual focal spot - the area of the target material being bombarded by electrons from the filament.البؤرة الفعلية: مساحة مادة الهدف التي تقصفها إلكترونات الفتيلة.
  • • Effective focal spot - the imaginary geometric line that can be drawn based on the actual focal spot size vs. the angle of the anode.البؤرة الفعّالة: الخط الهندسي التخيّلي المرسوم حسب حجم البؤرة الفعلية مقابل زاوية الأنود.

Any angle <45 degrees results in the effective FS being smaller than the actual FS, but 12 degrees target angle is the most common.

أي زاوية أقل من 45 درجة تجعل البؤرة الفعّالة أصغر من الفعلية، وزاوية الهدف 12 درجة هي الأشيع.

الفكرة المهمةزاوية أنود أصغر = بؤرة فعّالة أصغر = تفاصيل أفضل؛ والأشيع 12°.Smaller anode angle → smaller effective focal spot → better detail; 12° is most common.

تأثير الكعبAnode Heel Effect

An x-ray beam gets attenuated on the way out by the target material itself causing a decrease in intensity gradually from the cathode to anode direction as there is more of the target material to travel through. Therefore, the cathode side should be placed over the area of greatest density as this is the side with the most penetrating beam (احتمال خطأ: الأصح: الأعلى شدّة) .

حزمة الأشعة تتوهّن في طريق خروجها بمادة الهدف نفسها، فتقل شدتها تدريجياً من جهة الكاثود إلى جهة الأنود لأن الأشعة في جهة الأنود تعبر مادة هدف أكثر. لذلك يوضع جانب الكاثود فوق المنطقة الأكثر كثافة، لأنه الجانب ذو الحزمة الأكثر نفاذاً.

Decreasing the anode angle gives a smaller effective focal spot size, which is useful in imaging, but a larger anode heel effect. This results in a less uniform and more attenuated beam.

تقليل زاوية الأنود يعطي بؤرة فعّالة أصغر، وهذا مفيد بالتصوير، لكن تأثير الكعب يكون أكبر، فتصير الحزمة أقل انتظاماً وأكثر توهيناً.

smaller angle = smaller focal spot size but larger anode heel effect

زاوية أصغر = بؤرة أصغر لكن تأثير كعب أكبر.

الفكرة المهمةالشدة أعلى بجهة الكاثود ← نضع الكاثود على الجزء الأسمك.Intensity is higher on the cathode side: place the cathode over the thickest part.

الغلاف الزجاجي والغطاء الواقيGlass Envelope & Protective Housing

الغلاف الزجاجيGlass envelope

Is the container of the x – ray tube components , which supports the anode and cathode structures. which is usually made of Pyrex glass to enable it to withstand the tremendous heat generated, maintains a vacuum inside the tube, this vacuum allows for more efficient x-ray production and longer tube life. If the tube were filled with gas, the electron flow from cathode to anode would be reduced , fewer x – rays would be produced, and more heat would be created.

هو وعاء مكوّنات الأنبوب، ويسند تراكيب الأنود والكاثود. يُصنع عادةً من زجاج البايركس (Pyrex) حتى يتحمّل الحرارة الهائلة، ويحافظ على الفراغ داخل الأنبوب. والفراغ يجعل إنتاج الأشعة أكفأ ويطيل عمر الأنبوب؛ ولو امتلأ الأنبوب بالغاز لقلّ سريان الإلكترونات من الكاثود إلى الأنود، وقلّت الأشعة، وزادت الحرارة.

A recent improvement in tube design incorporates the metal rather than glass as part or all of the envelope. As glass envelope tubes age. Some tungsten vaporizes and coats the inside of the glass envelope. This alters the electric potential of the tube, allowing tube current to stray and interact with the glass envelope, the result is arcing and tube failure.

تحسين حديث في تصميم الأنابيب يستعمل المعدن بدل الزجاج في جزء من الغلاف أو كله. فمع تقادم الأنابيب الزجاجية يتبخّر بعض التنگستن ويغطّي داخل الغلاف، فيتغيّر الجهد الكهربائي للأنبوب ويتشتّت تيار الأنبوب ويتفاعل مع الزجاج، والنتيجة تفريغ كهربائي (arcing) وعطل الأنبوب.

Metal envelope tubes maintain a constant electric potential between the electrons of the tube current and the envelope. Therefore, they have longer life and are less likely to fail. Virtually all high – output x-ray tubes now use metal envelopes.

الأنابيب ذات الغلاف المعدني تحافظ على جهد كهربائي ثابت بين إلكترونات تيار الأنبوب والغلاف، لذلك عمرها أطول واحتمال عطلها أقل. وتقريباً كل الأنابيب عالية الناتج اليوم تستعمل أغلفة معدنية.

The tube window is a segment of the glass envelope, approximately 5 cm2 , that contains a thin section of glass through which the useful beam of x-rays is emitted. The thin window serves to allow maximum emission of x-rays with minimum absorption in the glass envelope.

نافذة الأنبوب (tube window) جزء من الغلاف الزجاجي مساحته حوالي 5 سم²، فيه زجاج رقيق تخرج منه الحزمة المفيدة؛ والنافذة الرقيقة تسمح بأكبر انبعاث للأشعة مع أقل امتصاص في الغلاف.

الغطاء الواقيProtective housing

The x – ray tube is always mounted inside a lead – lined protective housing designed to control two serious hazards that plagued early radiology

Cathode: The Filamentالكاثود: الفتيلة

Cathode: The Filament

The cathode is the negative side of the x-ray tube because it is a source of electrons and has two primary parts: filament & focusing cup.

Is a coil of wire usually about 2 mm in diameter and 1 to 2 cm long . An x – ray filament emit electrons when it heated. When the current through the filament sufficiently intense, approximately 4 A and above , the outer – shell electrons of the filament atoms are literally boiled off and ejected from the filament. this phenomenon is known as thermionic emission.

Filaments are usually made of thoriated tungsten, because its melting point is 3410 C°, and therefore it is not likely to burn-out. Also, tungsten doesn’t vaporize easily, if it did, the tube would quickly become gassy and its internal parts coated with tungsten.

This is the most common cause of tube failure, the addition of 1% to 2% thorium to the tungsten filament increases efficiency of thermionic emission and prolongs tube life.

Fig. (2) shows a magnified view of the cathode face and it shows dual filaments. Only one filament at a time will work. The small filament is designed to be used with relatively small parts while the large filament is used when larger body parts are being x-rayed.

The number of electrons that are released will directly influence the number of x-rays that are generated and therefore the dose of radiation also. Figure (3) shows how a low mA will result in fewer x-rays being produced and a high mA will result in a greater number of x-rays. The term mA is a unit of measure of electrical current that is used when the technologist selects the exposure factors.

By referring to the types of the filaments, the small filament directs its electron stream to the small focus while using the large filament, directs the stream over the larger focal spot. The small focal spot is superimposed over the large one.

Cathode: The Focusing Cupالكاثود: كأس التركيز

Cathode: The Focusing Cup

When the x-ray machine is powered up, electrons will literally “boil” off the filament. It glows white hot and the electrons hover around the filament in a “space charge” until the moment of exposure and then they accelerate very rapidly towards the anode which is not very far away. This process is known as “thermionic emission”. Thermionic emission occurs when the technologist begins to make an exposure by pressing the “ready” button on the machine.

The filament is embedded in a metal shroud called the focusing cup to eliminate the space charge and concentrate the electron stream in a pre-determined area on the anode target area known as a “focal spot”. Since all the electrons accelerated from cathode to anode are electrically negative, the beam tend to spread out owing to electrostatic repulsion and some electrons can even miss the anode completely , the focusing cup is negatively charged so that it condenses the electron beam to a small area of the anode, Figure (4) .The filament has its own circuit powered by a relatively low voltage and 4 – 6 amperes.

Anode & Targetالأنود والهدف

Anode & Target

The anode is the positive side of the x-ray tube, which divided into two types:

Stationary – anodes that are used in dental x-ray machine, some portable machines and other special purpose units in which high tube current and power are not required.

Rotating – anode which are used in general purpose x – ray tubes because they must be capable of producing high – intensity x – ray beams in a short time.

The rotor is actually part of a motor that is made to rotate at very high speed. In most x-ray tubes, the revolutions per minute is usually at 3,200 for standard technique settings, however if the technologist selects exposure factors that are considerably larger and therefore very hot, then the rotor can rotate at a much greater speed exceeding 5,000 rpm.

The rotation of the anode ensures that not any one spot will receive successive pulses of electrons. If there was no rotation, then it is very likely that the anode face would be damage due to high heat.

Rotating the anode allows a large surface area over which heat spread, providing an ability to tolerate greater heat deposition and to produce more x-ray photons per unit time compared with a fixed anode.

Target

The target is the area of the anode struck by the electrons from the cathode. In stationary – anode tubes the target consists of a tungsten – alloy metal embedded in the copper anode. In rotating – anode tubes the entire rotating disc is the target. Alloying the tungsten (usually with rhenium) gives it added mechanical strength to withstand the stress of the high rotation.

The benefits of using tungsten as a target material

Atomic number : Tungsten’s high atomic number ,74, results in higher – efficiency x – ray production and in higher – energy x – rays

Thermal conductivity : Tungsten has a thermal conductivity nearly equal to that of copper (احتمال خطأ: الأصح: أقل من نصف توصيل النحاس، مع أن بعض الكتب مثل Bushong تذكرها كما في المحاضرة) . It is therefore an efficient metal for dissipating the heat produced.

High melting point: Any material , if heated sufficiently , will melt and become liquid. Tungsten has a high melting point (3410 C°) and therefore can stand up under high tube current without pitting or bubbling.

Resists vaporization at high temperatures.

Its availability makes it cost-effective.

The anode target must be a good thermal conductor, when the electrons comprising the tube current slam into the anode, more than 99% of their kinetic energy is converted into heat. This heat must be conducted away quickly before it can melt the anode. copper is the most common anode material.

The distance between the cathode and the rotating anode disc is very close. This is designed this way to ensure that the projectile electron stream has a reasonably good chance of arriving at the anode in a relatively tight pattern. If the electrons were permitted to spread out, then the x-ray production process would become very inefficient

Line Focus Principleمبدأ البؤرة الخطية

Line Focus Principle

It is the angling of the anode target that makes the effective focal spot much smaller than the actual area of interaction.

The focal spot is the area of electron interaction and emanation of x-rays from the target surface.

Typical dimensions are nominal sizes of 1.0- to 1.2-mm (large) and 0.3- to 0.6-mm (small) focal spots, where nominal encompasses a range of focal spot sizes that are as acceptable according to manufacturer standards.

Ideally, the use of small focal spots is preferred to minimize geometric blurring of patient anatomy with magnification. However, the small focal area constrains x-ray tube output and heat loading factors, mainly due to heat concentrated in a small area.

Larger focal spots have higher instantaneous x-ray production capacity and are preferred, as long as blurring does not adversely affect resolution.

The focal spots which are imbedded in the rotating anode disc are angled in such a way that when the electrons strike the anode focal track, the angle makes it easier for the x-rays to be emitted in a downward direction. However depending on the angle of the anode face, this will change the appearance of the focal spot as seen from below or where the x-ray image receptor is positioned.

The anode angle and the resulting appearance of the focal spot as it would appear from the image receptor is known as the “Line Focus Principle’.

In the Fig. (7), note that as the angle of the anode decreases from 24 degrees to 12 degrees, the focal spot will appear smaller because you are looking up at it at an angle or obliquely. As the angle decreases, you actually see less of the focal spot dimensions and this will actually enhance the details recorded on the image. In general, the smaller the focal spot, the better the detail will be on the finished image.

Two types of focal spot

Actual focal spot - the area of the target material being bombarded by electrons from the filament.

Effective focal spot - the imaginary geometric line that can be drawn based on the actual focal spot size vs. the angle of the anode.

Any angle <45 degrees results in the effective FS being smaller than the actual FS, but 12 degrees target angle is the most common.

Anode Heel Effectتأثير الكعب

Anode Heel Effect

An x-ray beam gets attenuated on the way out by the target material itself causing a decrease in intensity gradually from the cathode to anode direction as there is more of the target material to travel through. Therefore, the cathode side should be placed over the area of greatest density as this is the side with the most penetrating beam (احتمال خطأ: الأصح: الأعلى شدّة) .

Decreasing the anode angle gives a smaller effective focal spot size, which is useful in imaging, but a larger anode heel effect. This results in a less uniform and more attenuated beam.

smaller angle = smaller focal spot size but larger anode heel effect

Glass Envelope & Protective Housingالغلاف الزجاجي والغطاء الواقي

Glass Envelope & Protective Housing

Glass envelope

Is the container of the x – ray tube components , which supports the anode and cathode structures. which is usually made of Pyrex glass to enable it to withstand the tremendous heat generated, maintains a vacuum inside the tube, this vacuum allows for more efficient x-ray production and longer tube life. If the tube were filled with gas, the electron flow from cathode to anode would be reduced , fewer x – rays would be produced, and more heat would be created.

A recent improvement in tube design incorporates the metal rather than glass as part or all of the envelope. As glass envelope tubes age. Some tungsten vaporizes and coats the inside of the glass envelope. This alters the electric potential of the tube, allowing tube current to stray and interact with the glass envelope, the result is arcing and tube failure.

Metal envelope tubes maintain a constant electric potential between the electrons of the tube current and the envelope. Therefore, they have longer life and are less likely to fail. Virtually all high – output x-ray tubes now use metal envelopes.

The tube window is a segment of the glass envelope, approximately 5 cm2 , that contains a thin section of glass through which the useful beam of x-rays is emitted. The thin window serves to allow maximum emission of x-rays with minimum absorption in the glass envelope.
