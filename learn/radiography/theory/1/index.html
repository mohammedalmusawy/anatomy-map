General Terms

Radiograph : An image of a patient's anatomic part(s), as produced by the action of

x-rays on an image receptor If the radiograph is produced with the use of traditional film-

screen technology, the image is stored and displayed on film; if the radiograph is produced

via digital technology, the image is viewed and stored with the use of computers.

Radiography : The process and procedures of producing a radiograph.

Radiograph versus x-ray film: In practice, the terms radiograph and x-ray film (or

just film) are often used interchangeably.

Image receptor (IR): The devicethat captures the radiographic image that exits the

patient; refers to both film-screen cassettes and digital acquisition devices.

Central ray (CR): Refers to the center-most portion of the x-ray beam emitted from

the x-ray tube; the portion of the x-ray beam that has the least divergence.

Directional terminology

These terms are essential for describing the relative

locations of different body structures.

1- Anterior (or ventral): Describes the front or

direction toward the front of the body.

2- Posterior (or dorsal): describes the back or

direction toward the back of the body.

3- Medial: toward the medial (midsagittal) plane.

4- Lateral: away from the medial (midsagittal) plane.

5- Superior: describes a position above or

higher than another part of the body proper.

6- Inferior: describes a position below or lower than another part of the

body proper.

7- Proximal: describes a position in a limb that is nearer to the point of

attachment or the trunk of the body.

8- Distal: describes a position in a limb that is farther from the point of

attachment or the trunk of the body.

9- Superficial: describes a position closer to the surface of the body. The

skin is superficial to the bones.

10- Deep: describes a position farther from the surface of the body. The

brain is deep to the skull.

11- Cranial/cephalic: toward the head.

12- Caudal: toward the feet.

وصف المصطلحات الاتجاهية

هذه المصطلحات أساسية لوصف المواقع النسبية لتراكيب الجسم المختلفة.

1- الأمامي (البطني): يصف الجهة الأمامية أو الاتجاه نحو مقدمة الجسم.

2- الخلفي (الظهري): يصف الجهة الخلفية أو الاتجاه نحو مؤخرة الجسم.

3- الإنسي: باتجاه المستوى الإنسي (السهمي الناصف).

4- الوحشي: بعيداً عن المستوى الإنسي (السهمي الناصف).

5- العلوي: موضع أعلى من جزء آخر من الجسم.

6- السفلي: موضع أدنى من جزء آخر من الجسم.

7- الدانِي (القريب): موضع في الطرف أقرب إلى نقطة الاتصال أو جذع الجسم.

8- القاصي (البعيد): موضع في الطرف أبعد عن نقطة الاتصال أو جذع الجسم.

9- السطحي: موضع أقرب إلى سطح الجسم؛ فالجلد سطحي بالنسبة للعظام.

10- العميق: موضع أبعد عن سطح الجسم؛ فالدماغ عميق بالنسبة للجمجمة.

11- القحفي/الرأسي: باتجاه الرأس.

12- الذيلي: باتجاه القدمين.

Planes of the body

Plane: straight line surface connecting two point. Three common planes as

used in medical imaging techniques.

1- The sagittal plane: divides the body or an organ vertically into right and

left sides. If this vertical plane runs directly down the middle of the body, it

is called the midsagittal or median plane. If it divides the body into

unequal right and left sides, it is called a parasagittal plane.

2- Coronal (frontal) plane: divides the body into an anterior part and a

posterior part.

3- Transverse or axial plane: divides the body into superior and inferior

part.
